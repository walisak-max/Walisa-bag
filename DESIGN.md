## Brand & Style
The design system embodies the warmth, deliberate pacing, and tactile prestige of an artisanal leather atelier. Built for discerning luxury connoisseurs who value craftsmanship, sustainable provenance, and timeless material beauty over fast fashion, the interface evokes calm intimacy, sensory depth, and grounded exclusivity.

The visual style blends **Tactile / Contemporary Editorial Luxury** with **Organic Minimalism**:
- Generous, serene whitespace rendered in warm stone and ecru rather than stark cold white.
- Physical material metaphors: delicate embossed divider lines, burnished metallic accents reminiscent of polished brass hardware, and supple layered cards mimicking cut leather edges.
- Unhurried, museum-grade typography where bilingual typesetting (Thai and English) pairs harmoniously across headline scales and micro-labels.
- Photography takes precedence: large-scale, tactile macro crops of edge-stitching, pebbled leather textures, and brass closures.

## Colors
The palette draws straight from the workshop: vegetable-tanned hides, fired terracotta clay, aged brass hardware, raw linen thread, and steeped espresso.

- **Primary (`#8C4327` — Terracotta Saddle):** Used for focal brand moments, primary CTAs, active indicators, and high-impact key badges.
- **Secondary (`#C69255` — Burnished Brass):** Applied to secondary highlights, decorative borders, active star ratings, foil-like badges, and subtle icon accents.
- **Tertiary (`#42281D` — Dark Espresso):** Replaces pure black entirely. Delivers grounding contrast across high-hierarchy typography, footers, structural outlines, and refined solid states.
- **Neutral (`#F7F4EE` — Warm Ecru Canvas):** The foundational canvas tint. Complemented by `#EDE7DC` for container surfaces and `#D8CEBF` for soft structural dividers.
- **Color Roles & States:**
  - High-emphasis text: `#2E1C14` (Deep Espresso)
  - Medium-emphasis text: `#6E5D53` (Warm Muted Umber)
  - Interactive hover state on Primary: `#70341E`
  - Subtle wash surface: `#F1ECE3`
  - Success/In-Stock: `#4A6044` (Olive Laurel)
  - Warning/Limited Edition: `#BA6E2B` (Amber Ochre)

## Typography
The typographic pairing reflects a dialogue between old-world editorial craftsmanship and contemporary clarity.

- **Headings (Playfair Display):** Conveys the poise, heritage, and romantic lyricism of haute couture editorial spreads. It preserves delicate serifs and high stroke contrast.
- **Body & Labels (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Selected for warm, geometric precision with wide apertures and clean proportions that ensure exceptional legibility at small sizes and smooth bilingual integration alongside loopless modern Thai typefaces (such as Prompt or Sukhumvit Set).
- **Bilingual Typographic Harmony:** When rendering Thai copy within Playfair Display heading blocks, fallback to a refined humanist Thai display typeface with an equivalent optical baseline. In body contexts, Plus Jakarta Sans provides effortless height matching with 1.5x to 1.6x line heights to honor Thai tone marks and ascenders without vertical clipping.
- **Letter Spacing:** All uppercase micro-labels (`label-sm`, `label-md`) strictly mandate generous positive tracking (`0.06em` to `0.1em`) to mimic atelier hot-stamped leather monogramming.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout uses a fixed-fluid hybrid grid anchored to a standard 12-column responsive architecture on desktop (max content width `1360px`), transitioning to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile.

- **Rhythm & Cadence:** Generous, breathable intervals dictate the viewing experience. Products are given editorial staging space rather than dense e-commerce card stuffing.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - `Desktop (>= 1024px)`: 12 columns, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 3rem` (expanding up to auto-centered canvas).
  - `Tablet (768px - 1023px)`: 8 columns, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 2rem`.
  - `Mobile (<= 767px)`: 4 columns, `gutter: 1rem`, `margin: 1.25rem`.
- **Reflow & Asymmetry:** Showcase sections feature staggered alternating spans (e.g., 7 columns for artisanal editorial imagery, 5 columns for bespoke bag specifications and storytelling) that stack gracefully to single-column full-bleed blocks on mobile.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is warm, tactile, and naturalistic, avoiding clinical digital dropshadows. Visual layers emulate supple leather panels resting on woven canvas work surfaces.

- **Ambient Warm Shadows:** Shadows employ a dual-stop projection tinted with Deep Espresso (`#42281D`):
  - *Resting Card Elevation:* `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(66, 40, 29, 0.05), 0 1px 3px rgba(66, 40, 29, 0.04)`
  - *Hover / Floating Elevation:* `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(66, 40, 29, 0.09), 0 2px 6px rgba(66, 40, 29, 0.04)`
  - *Modal / Drawer Elevation:* `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(66, 40, 29, 0.16)`
- **Tonal Layering & Soft Outlines:** Cards and modals sit over `#F7F4EE` canvas with `#FFFFFF` or `#EDE7DC` surfaces, bounded by a 1px micro-border of `#E3DACD` (or `#C69255` at 25% opacity for brass highlights).
- **Frosted Artisanal Glass:** Used exclusively on floating navigation and checkout drawers: `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` over `rgba(247, 244, 238, 0.82)` with a delicate hairline bottom stroke in `#D8CEBF`.

## Shapes
The shape language mirrors the gentle, tailored contours of bespoke leather goods—softened radii, curved flap silhouettes, and sculpted handles.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px`):** Used for interactive controls, inputs, dropdown menus, and utility buttons.
- **Medium Radius (`1rem` / `16px`):** Used for standard handbag product cards, lookbook tiles, and testimonial cards.
- **Large Organic Radius (`1.5rem` / `24px`):** Applied to modal dialogs, atelier spotlight banners, and full-width gallery image containers.
- **Pill Exception:** Swatches, tag badges, and quick-filter pills utilize fully rounded stadium shapes (`9999px`) to echo saddle-stitched strap loops.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Terracotta Saddle (`#8C4327`) fill with crisp Warm Ecru (`#F7F4EE`) text. Radius `8px`. Gentle expansion hover with `#70341E` background and subtle brass focus ring (`2px` solid `#C69255` offset by `2px`).
- **Secondary (Brass Outline):** Border `1.5px` solid `#C69255`, Dark Espresso (`#42281D`) typography, transparent background. On hover, fills with `#C69255` at 10% opacity.
- **Tertiary / Text:** Dark Espresso with letter-spaced `label-lg` caps and an animated underline in `#8C4327` that transitions smoothly from 0% to 100% width on hover.

### Product Cards
- Warm ecru background (`#FFFFFF` on `#F7F4EE` canvas), radius `16px`, soft warm shadow.
- Image container features subtle `4:5` vertical aspect ratio with a soft `#F1ECE3` backing fill to make leather pieces stand out.
- Top-right corner includes a discreet circular heart icon button with a faint brass outline.
- Includes dual-line title: Playfair Display model name accompanied by leather grade and colorway metadata in Plus Jakarta Sans.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Chips (e.g., "Handcrafted", "Limited Edition"):** Pill-shaped, soft linen background (`#EDE7DC`), Espresso text (`label-md`), bordered by a `1px` subtle brass stroke (`#C69255` at 30%).
- **Color/Leather Swatches:** Circular 28px swatches surrounded by an active double-ring: outer `#8C4327` hairline with a 2px spacing gap.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px` solid `#D8CEBF`, radius `8px`, typography `body-md` in `#42281D`.
- Focus state: border color `#8C4327`, paired with a delicate `0 0 0 3px rgba(140, 67, 39, 0.12)` ambient halo.
- Labels float above in `label-md` tracking uppercase Dark Espresso.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkbox squares (`radius 4px`) and Radio circles adopt `#D8CEBF` border in unselected state.
- Checked state: Terracotta Saddle (`#8C4327`) fill with an engraved Ecru checkmark or brass central dot.

### Specialized Boutique Components
- **Language Switcher (TH / EN):** Segmented pill control in navigation bar with smooth sliding warm leather indicator.
- **Leather & Hardware Care Accordion:** Clean hairline dividers (`#D8CEBF`), warm serif trigger headers, and collapsible organic panels.
- **Artisan Monogram Previewer:** Interactive input that previews blind debossing or gold-foil stamping directly onto the handbag image canvas.
